# ConZ admin guide (Option A), draft 05/11/2026

Describes what the admin screens do today. Money actions need a recent Google sign-in (step-up) and are written to the audit log. Only the two owners are super admins.

## Daily checks
1. **Admin home, "Needs attention":** open jobs that reached no driver, pickup checks (photo over 2 km from the supplier, or no photo location), disputes.
2. **Verifications:** review ID, licence and truck before approving a driver. Use the verification screen, never edit approval fields directly. A truck change sends the driver back to review.
3. **Top-ups queue (Revenue):** approve or reject manual top-ups; confirm the money really arrived first. Paynow top-ups arrive on their own.
4. **Disputes:** open the panel for photos with GPS, delivery distance, route trail and chat transcript before deciding.

## Money screens
- **Revenue:** commission rate (7%), top-up and withdrawal queues, CSV export. Withdrawals are switched off in Option A.
- **Ledger:** every wallet and platform movement. Entries cannot be edited or deleted.
- **Wallet adjustments:** adjust/reverse and credit requests use two people (request, then a different admin decides). Always write the reason.
- **Settings (super admin only):** diesel price, price multiplier, fuel assumptions, material prices. Each change needs the step-up and a reason. Diesel stays $1.87 unless the owners decide.
- **Reconciliation report:** lists payments that did not settle cleanly. Check it daily while Paynow is live.

## Rules
- Never change the commission rate while jobs are active unless 0113 is live (until then a mid-job change charges a different amount from the reservation).
- First completed job per driver is commission-free; do not "fix" a $0 charge on a first job.
- Do not turn on withdrawals, promotional credit, points or escrow without an owner decision.
- Customers pay drivers directly; ConZ does not hold or guarantee customer payments. Do not promise otherwise in chat or disputes.

## Who to call
Owners for any money discrepancy, any admin account change, or a suspected fraud.
