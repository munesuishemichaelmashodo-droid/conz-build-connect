## Where Con Z is different (true today, from this repo)

- **The truck and the material in one job, priced from the road route.** BuildMate also quotes a delivered
  price; ShopAround and BuilderPal stop at quotes.
- **Drivers compete.** Customers see several offers, each with pickup time and truck, and can counter
  (BuildMate sets one price).
- **Live tracking, a delivery PIN, and photo evidence with GPS at pickup and drop-off,** plus an admin dispute
  panel. None of the four home pages mentions tracking or delivery proof.
- **Open requests:** when no listed supplier covers a job, a driver can propose a source (PR #31, not merged).
  It is labelled "not checked by Con Z". An optional "Ask Con Z for a quote" exists behind a switch (off).

## Where they are ahead (worth copying, honestly)

1. **Upfront delivered price with no wait** (BuildMate). Con Z's verified estimate already does this where a
   supplier is listed; the gap is supplier coverage and gate prices (see `SUPPLIER-CANDIDATES-2026-10-26.md`).
2. **Payment options named at the price** (BuildMate). Con Z supports paying the driver directly; say so next
   to the price.
3. **Tiered supplier verification shown on the badge** (BuilderPal). Con Z has verified/unverified only; a
   visible "what we checked" line would be more honest than a single badge.
4. **Materials estimator** (BuildMate, BuilderPal). Con Z asks for m³; a "how much do I need?" helper would help
   first-time builders.
5. **WhatsApp as a channel** (BuildMate, BuilderPal). Con Z sends no WhatsApp; this needs the owner's decision
   (cost, and the no-paid-SMS rule).

No fake reviews, counts or badges should be added to compete. Every item above uses data Con Z already has
or would collect.
